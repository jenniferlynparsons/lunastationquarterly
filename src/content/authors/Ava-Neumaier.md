Ava Neumaier (she/her) is a speculative fiction writer from New York completing her degree at the University of Iowa. With a passion for mythology and female-led stories, she enjoys exploring the intersection between the otherworldly and the everyday. She is a 2023 American Voices Award nominee, a 2026 Fulbright nominee, and is the co-executive editor of earthwords literary magazine at UIowa.
