Kristine Chang is a Chinese-American writer based in San Francisco. Her work has appeared or is forthcoming in Panorama, Full House Literary, Sine Theta Magazine, and the San Diego Zine Festival. She is 2026 Rooted and Written Fellow. She loves cooking and making ice cream.
