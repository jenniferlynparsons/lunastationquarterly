KT Reed grew up in Montana and lives in Seattle. She is a social justice lawyer by day, where she has worked in public defense and tenants’ rights. KT lives with chronic vestibular migraines, and she can be found going on slow, dizzy walks through every kind of nature. She was a graduate of Hugo House’s Yearlong in Speculative Fiction in 2024 and is currently writing a queer speculative fiction novel. You can find her at KTReedwrites.com.
