Francesca Forrest is the author of the Tales of the Polity, which include The Inconvenient God (2018), Lagoonfire (2021), and A Flash of Scarlet (forthcoming 2027), all from Annorlunda Books. She has also published a number of short stories, including “Semper Vivens” (Andromeda Spaceways, 2024) and “The Bee Wife” (self-published, 2025). She enjoys learning languages and would like to learn a word or two in yours.
