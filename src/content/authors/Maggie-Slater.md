Maggie Slater’s speculative fiction has appeared in Apex Magazine, Science Fiction World, and Redivider, among other venues. She lives in an 1800s farmhouse in New England (USA) with two half-tamed boys, one half-trained dog, her husband, her parents, and at least one benign ghost. When she has an almost quiet moment, she enjoys Haruki Murakami novels, sampling craft beer, and hoarding cheap notebooks. For more information about her and her current projects, visit her blog at maggieslater.com or find her on Instagram: @maggiedot_writes .
