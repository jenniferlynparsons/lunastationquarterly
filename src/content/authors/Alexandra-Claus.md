Alexandra Claus (she/her) is a thirty-something writer and Sometimes Adult, dabbling in fantasy, science fiction, and the occasional angsty poem. She grew up in the Midwest, amid cornfields and soybeans, and often dreamed her backyard was the entrance to a thousand fantastical worlds. Now she resides in the Pacific Northwest with her husband, daughter, and two ridiculously cute dogs, and still finds herself dreaming up fantastical, impossible, and magical worlds.
