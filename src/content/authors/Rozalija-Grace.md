Rozalija Elizaveta Grace is a Russian diasporic poet and fabulist from southern Alaska whose work exploring exile, intimacy, language, and faith has been nominated for the Pushcart Prize and featured in Dappled Things, Rust & Moth, Room, and other journals. Her translations of gay, women’s, and minority voices in early Soviet poetry have been nominated for Best Literary Translations. She currently serves as a poetry editor for Psaltery & Lyre and lives in Minneapolis with her Hungarian American novelist partner (the real genius) and her dombyra (the real Qazaq). Read more at her website and on Bluesky @rozalijagrace.com.
