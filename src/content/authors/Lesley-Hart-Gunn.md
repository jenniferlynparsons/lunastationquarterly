Lesley Hart Gunn is a Rhysling nominated poet with publications in Strange Horizons, Asimov’s Science Fiction, Uncanny, and others. She is originally from Nova Scotia, Canada but currently teaches college writing at Utah Valley University where she lives with her partner, three children and two cats. She occasionally haunts Instagram at medusamakesnoise.
