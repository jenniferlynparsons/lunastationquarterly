Emma McCoy (M.A) has two poetry books: This Voice Has an Echo (Solum Press, 2024) and In Case I Live Forever (Alien Buddha Press, 2022). She’s been published in places like Vast Chasm Magazine, Stirring Literary, and Across the Margin. She’s the operations editor of Vessels of Light and reads for Whale Road Review. Catch her on Substack: https://poetrybyemma.substack.com/
