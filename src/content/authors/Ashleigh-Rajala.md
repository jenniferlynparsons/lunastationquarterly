An award-winning fiction writer, erstwhile filmmaker, and sometime zinester, Ashleigh Rajala lives and works on the Canadian side of the Pacific Northwest, on the traditional territories of the Coast Salish peoples. Her work has been published in numerous journals and anthologies, including Room, Redwing, Quarter Castle, and SAD MAG. She also designs role-playing and murder mystery games under her label Minor Arcana Games.
