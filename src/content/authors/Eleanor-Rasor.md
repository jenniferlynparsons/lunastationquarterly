Eleanor Rasor is a fantasy writer based in New England. She studied English and history at Mount Holyoke College and currently works as a bookseller. She is also the author of the young adult fantasy novel Twelve Dead Princesses and volunteers as a slush reader for GigaNotoSaurus. She loves fairy tales, learning obscure facts about history, and swimming in the ocean.
