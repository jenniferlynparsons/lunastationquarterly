Spoorthi AG is an Engineering new grad,  from South India who writes fiction in the margins of everything else. Her passion for storytelling has always run alongside the life that practical circumstances built for her, sometimes in tension with it, sometimes because of it. She believes the best stories are the ones that are imperfect and alive, that carry depth without apology. She is also at work on her first fantasy novel and writes poetry that breaks things and puts them back together.
