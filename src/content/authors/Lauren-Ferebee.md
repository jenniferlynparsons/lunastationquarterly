Lauren is a multi-genre writer who’s newish to fiction with previous publications in Flash Fiction Online, Tiny Molecules, and The Future Fire. She’s an award-winning playwright, and screenwriter with TV/film projects in development. She's also an independent theatre artist whose work has been produced in Chicago and New York and supported by The New Harmony Project, The Kimmel Harding Nelson Center, Saltonstall, and HUB-BUB. Her favorite play is Three Sisters (Chekhov), thanks for asking. MFA/playwriting: University of Arkansas.
